7. Storage: valid owner upload attaches a normalized private image; forged type/oversize/cross-user

8. Jobs: paid order and outbox committed together, dispatch delivered, receipt present. Replay

9. Telemetry: controlled event appears at the exact ID in the separate Sentry project; no account,

10. Runtime limits: inspect connection/worker counts and host quota; verify reconnect/retry after

11. Recovery: retained backup restored into a separate database; old app compatible with expanded
    schema. Record backup age and possible lost writes before a real rollback decision.
